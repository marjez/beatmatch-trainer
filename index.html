Crate & settings
Crate

Load your own tracks — they're stored on this device and stay in your crate between sessions. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. BPM is read from the filename (e.g. "Track 128bpm.mp3") or detected automatically; tap a value to correct it. Select crate.json along with the audio and playback starts on a real downbeat instead of in the intro.

Sixteen Creative Commons house, tech-house and minimal tracks from netlabels on the Internet Archive, ~250 MB, downloaded once and kept on this device. Tempos and beat grids are pre-analysed. Credits are listed below the crate.

Mode
Visual aids adds what Beatmatch PRO puts on screen: level meters, scrolling waveforms with the beat grid, and a score out of 100 with stars on the reveal. No BPM is ever shown while you play — a number to read is a match you didn't hear. Ears only is the honest default: with meters and waveforms you can line the kicks up by eye.
Split pans deck 1 hard left and deck 2 hard right — one ear on the monitor, like cueing on the mixer. ×2 opens both pitch faders to ±16% when a pair needs the room. Hold nudge to bend tempo like touching the platter. Cue: tap while playing to jump back to the start, or hold while stopped to preview. Grab the pitch knob and drag; slide well sideways while dragging for fine control (the readout turns amber).
Fallback: starts both decks on one audio-clock tick, phrase-aligned. Real decks are started one at a time with Play and Cue, which is what the mixer screen is for.
